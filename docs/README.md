# docs/

Notes and manuscripts. Conventions:

- **Dated working notes** (`<topic>_YYYY-MM-DD.md`) are where results land
  first — they are the provenance record and the place corrections land first.
  A result is not *recorded* until its full proof is transcribed into
  [`../master.tex`](../master.tex) (main line) or
  [`extended_manuscript.md`](extended_manuscript.md) (alternative paths).
- The standing documents here — [`latest_state.md`](latest_state.md),
  [`evidence_ledger.md`](evidence_ledger.md),
  [`program_map.md`](program_map.md),
  [`extended_manuscript.md`](extended_manuscript.md) — are rewritten in place
  as the programme moves; superseded notes go to `../old/`, not to the bin.
- `program_map.html` is generated from `program_map.md` by
  `../tools/render_docs.py` — never hand-edit it.
